ClearSpeak beacon — breadboard layout

Particle Argon · 2 LEDs · 2 resistors · no jumper wires required

How to place it

1. Push the Argon in so it straddles the middle channel, with the header printed GND A0 A1 A2 A3 along the bottom.
2. Red LED: long leg in the A0 column, short leg in the A1 column.
3. Resistor from the A1 column to the GND column.
4. Second LED: long leg in A2, short leg in A3.
5. Second resistor from A3 to GND.

Why it works without wires

Every column of five holes is one electrical connection, so a pin and a component lead in the same column are joined. The LED legs and resistor leads are the wiring.

A1 and A3 are only meeting points for two leads. The firmware never drives them, so they sit as unused inputs.

Watch for

LED polarity. Long leg towards the A pin, short leg towards the resistor. Backwards means no light and no harm.

Resistor value. Anything from 220 Ω to 1 kΩ is fine on 3.3 V. Direction does not matter.

Antenna. Leave it connected and do not pull it off. Bluetooth uses the on-board antenna, not that one.
